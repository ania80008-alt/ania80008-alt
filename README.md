Hi, I'm Anita.
I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, and my academic interests lie at the intersection of neuroscience, computer science, and artificial intelligence. I am fascinated by the brain as a complex information-processing system and by the possibility of understanding, modeling, and eventually reproducing aspects of its remarkable capabilities through computational and engineering approaches.
My research interests span computational neuroscience, cognitive neuroscience, and NeuroAI, with particular curiosity about learning and memory, perception and visual processing, emotion, cognition, and the neural mechanisms underlying human behavior. I am also interested in how computational methods can contribute to understanding neurological disorders, including Alzheimer's disease, and in how insights from biological neural systems can inspire new artificial intelligence architectures and brain-inspired technologies.
More broadly, I am drawn to questions about how the brain represents information, forms and retrieves memories, interprets sensory input, and generates complex experiences. I hope to explore these questions through interdisciplinary research combining computational modeling, machine learning, neural data analysis, and neuroscience.
